Know Your Materials

The Livna material library.

Understanding fabric is the foundation of understanding fashion. Here's what every client should know.

Silk

Natural · Protein fibre

+

Produced by silkworms, silk is the most luxurious natural fibre and the one most responsive to dye. It drapes with a fluid intelligence no synthetic can replicate, and possesses a natural sheen that catches light differently depending on movement.

OccasionEvening · Bridal · Formal
CareDry clean or hand wash cold
WeightLightweight to medium
Best forDraped silhouettes, gowns

Wool Crepe

Natural · Animal fibre

+

Wool crepe is the tailor's ideal canvas — firm enough to hold a sharp silhouette, yet yielding enough to move naturally with the body. Its slight texture absorbs light rather than reflecting it, giving garments a quiet authority.

OccasionCorporate · Formal · Tailoring
CareDry clean recommended
WeightMedium to heavy
Best forSuits, structured coats

Velvet

Woven · Cut-pile

+

Velvet's pile creates a directional sheen that changes as the wearer moves — making it one of the most visually dynamic fabrics in existence. At Livna, we use silk-backed velvet for evening pieces and cotton velvet for structured separates.

OccasionEvening · Winter formal
CareDry clean only
WeightMedium to heavy
Best forCapes, jackets, gowns

Linen

Natural · Bast fibre

+

Linen is the most honest fabric — it shows every crease and wears every wash with a graceful patina. Lightweight and breathable, it is indispensable for warm-climate dressing and the foundation of Livna's resort and casual couture collections.

OccasionCasual · Resort · Daywear
CareMachine wash or hand wash
WeightLightweight
Best forCo-ords, shirts, wide trousers

Aso-Oke

Handloom · Yoruba heritage

+

Aso-Oke (àṣọ oke — "top cloth" in Yoruba) is hand-woven in narrow strips on a loom, then joined to form fabric. Each weaver's work is distinctive. The fabric carries ritual significance and is traditionally worn for celebrations. At Livna, we work directly with master weavers in Iseyin, Oyo State.

OccasionCultural ceremonies · Celebrations
CareDry clean or gentle hand wash
WeightMedium
Best forGeles, agbadas, modern silhouettes

Duchess Satin

Woven · Heavyweight

+

Duchess satin has a structured body that holds shape without relying entirely on internal boning. Its high lustre and smooth surface make it the classical choice for bridal and formal evening wear. It photographs exceptionally — the texture reads as pure light.

OccasionBridal · Formal · Evening
CareDry clean only
WeightHeavy
Best forBridal gowns, ball skirts

Atelier Advice

Style guides.

I

Dressing for Your Body — Not a Body Type

The fashion industry spent decades mapping human bodies onto five rigid categories. We reject this entirely. At Livna, we design for the specific proportions, preferences, and personality of one person: you.

The key questions are: Where do you want attention drawn? What do you want concealed — not because it should be, but because you prefer it? What silhouettes make you feel like your best self? These questions yield a brief far more useful than any body-type chart.

Key principles

  • Commission for your current body, not the body you plan to have
  • Consider the event, not just the dress code
  • The best fit is one you forget you're wearing
  • One perfectly fitting piece outperforms ten approximate ones
II

Building a Bespoke Wardrobe — A Long-term Strategy

A wardrobe built from bespoke pieces functions entirely differently from one assembled from high street or even luxury ready-to-wear. Each piece was made to work specifically with your body, your colouring, your lifestyle — which means they naturally work with each other.

The approach: begin with occasion. What three events in the next 12 months require you to look extraordinary? Commission for those. Over time, the gaps reveal themselves.

Where to begin

  • Commission your highest-stakes piece first
  • Neutrals and monochromes travel furthest in a wardrobe
  • Invest in construction, not embellishment
  • Keep your measurement card updated after each piece
