Before you ask

Common questions.

How long does a commission take?

Timelines vary by complexity. A tailored suit takes 3–4 weeks; an elaborate gown, 8–12 weeks. We'll confirm your timeline during the initial consultation. Rush commissions are sometimes possible for an additional fee.

Do I need exact measurements?

Not to begin. Our initial form accepts estimates. Once we confirm your commission, we'll schedule a measurement session in-person (Lagos) or provide a precise guide for remote clients. Final fittings are included.

What if I'm outside Lagos?

We commission for clients across Africa and internationally. Remote consultations are conducted via video call. Measurements are taken locally using our guide, and pieces are shipped securely with full insurance.

Can I see fabric samples?

Yes. After your initial consultation, we'll send fabric swatches for your selection. For international clients, we courier a sample kit. The final material selection happens before cutting begins.

What is the deposit structure?

A 50% deposit is required to begin work, with the balance due before delivery. Deposits are non-refundable once cutting begins. We accept bank transfers (Nigerian and international) and selected digital payment methods.

Do you make alterations after delivery?

We offer one complimentary alteration session within 30 days of delivery for fit adjustments. Every piece is documented with full measurements, so future alterations — or a repeat commission — are always possible.
